Data Collection and Usage

The Application does not require user registration or account creation. The Service Provider does not collect or sell personally identifiable information (such as your legal name, email address, phone number, or persistent advertising identifiers).

To provide core interactive features, the Application processes real-time device location and transmits it during active sessions to functional external APIs, as detailed below.

Device Permissions and Augmented Reality (AR)

To deliver its interactive features and Augmented Reality (AR) mechanics, the Application requests the following permissions:

  • Camera: The camera feed is accessed in real time exclusively to enable Augmented Reality tracking and visual features. The camera stream is processed locally and ephemerally in volatile memory (RAM). The Application does not record, take photos, store, or transmit any images or video feeds outside your device.
  • Location (GPS): Location data is requested to contextualize in-game mechanics relative to the user's geographic area. During runtime, geographic coordinates are held temporarily in memory and transmitted to functional external APIs solely to retrieve contextual game data and regional features. The Application does not track your location in the background when the app is closed, nor does it create persistent travel history profiles.

Third-Party Services and APIs

The Application utilizes standard third-party platform libraries and functional APIs to operate correctly:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)
  • External Functional APIs: Real-time coordinates are transmitted to external service APIs exclusively to provide in-game environmental data and geographic features. These requests do not transmit personal identifiers.

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

The Application does not use generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies or automated decision-making/profiling to process personal data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Location data is retained ephemerally in active memory only for the duration of the current application session to perform necessary API requests. The Service Provider does not maintain databases storing historical location trails linked to individual users.

How to delete your data: Because data is handled in runtime memory and not stored on persistent remote databases, closing or uninstalling the Application immediately halts all processing. You can also revoke location or camera permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at walter.franklin@ufpe.br.

User Rights (GDPR & LGPD)

Under applicable data protection laws (including the GDPR and the Brazilian LGPD), you have rights regarding your personal data. Because the Application processes session-based location data without associating it with personal identifiers, exercising rights of access or erasure is effectively fulfilled by terminating use or revoking device permissions. For inquiries, you may contact the Data Controller at the email provided above.
